# Quick help

These instructions follow the controls in `app/src/map/CompareSites.tsx`, `app/src/map/SiteFinder.tsx`, `app/src/sun/SunPanel.tsx`, and `app/src/pod/PodLogPanel.tsx`.

## Compare two sites

1. Open Compare sites from the menu.
2. Choose Location A and Location B using the maps, saved locations, or coordinates. Set both worlds to Earth to compare two Earth sites.
3. Choose the Earth month and click Compare selected locations.
4. Read the conditions and differences in the table below.

## Find test sites

1. Open Find test sites.
2. Choose a Mission target, Equipment test, and Test month.
3. Open a ranked area to see its conditions, match scores, and missing measurements.

## Compare sunlight

1. Open Sunlight.
2. Choose a target, Earth latitude, and Earth month.
3. Compare the Sky view or Elevation chart. Use the playback controls to move through the samples.

## Open field measurements

1. Open Field measurements.
2. Drop a sensor-pod CSV onto the page or choose a file.
3. Read the charts comparing the recording with satellite and rover data. Simulated recordings are labelled simulated.

## Website overview

Find Earth locations for testing Moon and Mars equipment. Start with Find test sites, or open Compare sites to inspect two locations side by side.
