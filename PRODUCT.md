# Proving Ground

## Register

product

## Users

Engineers and mission planners comparing Earth locations and months for field tests of Moon and Mars equipment. The primary setting is a laptop workspace, with mobile access in the field. Source: `docs/project-brief.md` and `docs/mission-plan.md`.

## Product Purpose

Help a planner choose a target, test and month, inspect ranked Earth areas, and understand the evidence and limits behind each match. Connect satellite estimates with sensor-pod observations without changing their provenance.

## Brand Personality

Precise, approachable, exploratory. The owner requested a redesign inspired by Croptions and delegated design decisions after project discovery on 15 November 2026.

## Anti-references

Avoid a promotional landing page that delays the tools, an ornamental mission-control dashboard, fabricated telemetry, and scores without coverage or limitations. Croptions informs workflow clarity and grouping, rather than its farming imagery or copy.

## Design Principles

- Put the planning task ahead of presentation.
- Give geography space and keep results beside the map.
- Make the next action clear without hiding scientific caveats.
- Keep estimates, measurements, simulations and missing values distinct.
- Use one consistent visual vocabulary across every tool.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, labelled controls, reduced-motion support, and layouts that work on narrow screens. Keep scientific units, legends and source attribution visible. No additional audience-specific requirements were supplied.
